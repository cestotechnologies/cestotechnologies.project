01 Overview

A Scalable International
Wellness Brand System (PK + UK).

Hikmabiotics is a premium natural supplements brand offering herbal wellness products, including shilajit. The build focused on two localized websites, dual social identities, and a performance-driven launch in Pakistan — while keeping one unified premium brand voice.

Industry

Natural supplements, wellness, and herbal products.

  • Premium positioning
  • Education-led buying
  • Trust and compliance needs

Scope

Dual websites, social, organic content, and Meta Ads.

  • PK + UK domains
  • Dual social presence
  • Organic brand building
  • Meta Ads purchase funnel (PK)

Objectives

Localization, scalability, and early sales validation.

  • Two market-specific websites
  • Localized social content
  • PK paid launch validation
  • UK organic authority foundation

UK Website

Premium minimal design with compliance-ready product pages.

  • Premium, minimal aesthetic
