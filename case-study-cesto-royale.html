01 Overview

A Premium Shopify Store
for Multiple Product Categories.

Cesto Royale was created as a modern ecommerce brand offering a broad selection of consumer products through one unified Shopify storefront. The objective was to create a store that feels premium, easy to browse, and simple to purchase from — while supporting different product categories under one consistent brand identity.

Industry

Ecommerce / dropshipping across several consumer categories.

  • Home & kitchen
  • Health & beauty
  • Car accessories

  • Lifestyle products

End-to-end Shopify store development.

  • Navigation structure
  • Mobile optimization

  • App integrations

Focus

A premium shopping experience, kept simple.

02 Challenge

Building One Premium Store
Across Multiple Categories.

Multi-category stores can quickly become cluttered and difficult to navigate. The challenge was to organize a wide product range while keeping the shopping experience clean, consistent, and easy for customers to understand.

Multiple Product Categories

The store needed to support very different product types without feeling disconnected.

  • Clear category structure
  • Consistent branding
  • Easy browsing

Mobile Shopping Experience

A large portion of ecommerce traffic comes from mobile, so the store needed to stay simple on smaller screens.

  • Responsive layouts
  • Mobile-friendly menus
  • Clear product CTAs

Product Trust

Dropshipping stores need stronger presentation and trust signals to help customers feel comfortable purchasing.

  • Product information
  • Delivery messaging
  • Secure checkout
  • Clear policies

Scalable Store Structure

The store needed to support new products and categories without a complete redesign.

  • Flexible collections
  • Reusable product layouts
  • Scalable navigation
