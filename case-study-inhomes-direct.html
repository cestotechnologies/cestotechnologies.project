01 Overview

A Premium Interiors Store
With Real-World Ordering Logic.

InHomes Direct sells flooring, worktops, and interior products — where customers think in area (m²), packs, and pieces, and sometimes need a survey for accurate estimating. The goal was to unify these product types into one seamless storefront.

Industry

Home interiors, sold direct to UK consumers.

  • Flooring & accessories
  • Worktops
  • Tiles
  • Beds
  • Doors & windows
  • Lighting

Scope

Custom storefront plus the product logic behind it.

  • Calculators
  • Product templates
  • Policies by type
  • Custom forms

Constraint

No paid calculator or logic apps — built entirely in custom code.

  • Lightweight custom code
  • Fast UX
  • Scalable rules
02 Challenge

Standard Ecommerce Doesn't
Handle Flooring & Worktops.

Unlike normal retail, these products require measurement-based buying and category-specific policies. Off-the-shelf apps were expensive, rigid, and slowed performance.

Packs vs. Area

Flooring is sold in packs, but customers order by square metres. This needs accurate conversion and rounding.

  • Area → packs
  • Round-up logic
  • Avoid under-ordering

Worktops Need a Survey

Worktops cannot be priced instantly. Customers need an estimate range and a survey request flow.

  • Starting-from indication
  • Survey request
  • Measurement submission

Mixed Unit Products

One store must support per-piece items, per-area items, and estimated items with different UI patterns.

  • Per piece
  • Per area
  • Estimated / quote-based

Policies by Product Type

Shipping and returns differ by category; the store must display the correct policy per product.

  • Different shipping rules
  • Different returns rules
  • Clear messaging
03 Solution

A Custom Logic Engine
Built Into the Storefront.

We implemented lightweight, custom-coded calculators and conditional product templates. This delivered a fast UX and avoided paid calculator apps entirely.

  • Real-time calculators
  • Conditional product layouts
  • Category-based policies
  • No paid apps

Flooring Logic

Area (m²) input → packs required → rounded up → real-time total price, calculated live as the customer types.

Worktop Logic

Shows only a percentage / starting-from indication, and requires a survey submission for the final estimate.

Unified Unit Handling

Per-piece, per-area, and estimated products all run through one storefront, without splitting into separate sites.

Custom Forms

Purpose-built forms for survey requests and measurement data collection, with shipping and returns shown dynamically by category.
