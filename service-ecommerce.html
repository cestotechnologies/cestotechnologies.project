01 Overview

Shopify & WordPress

Shopify and WordPress power most of the web for good reason. The problems usually come from bloated themes, a pile of plugins nobody audits, and a checkout that was never tested on a phone.

We build and repair on both: custom themes rather than marketplace bloat, migrations that keep your URLs and rankings, and speed work that shows up in the conversion rate.
